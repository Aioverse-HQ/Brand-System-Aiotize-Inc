# Logo Usage

The Aiotize logo is the most recognizable expression of our brand. Consistent, correct usage builds recognition and trust.

> 📁 Logo source files: [`brand/logo/`](../brand/logo/)

---

## 🎨 Logo Variants

| Variant | File | When to Use |
|---------|------|-------------|
| **Primary (color)** | `aiotize-logo-primary.svg` | Default — use on white or light backgrounds |
| **Reversed (white)** | `aiotize-logo-reversed.svg` | Use on dark or brand-color backgrounds |
| **Monochrome (black)** | `aiotize-logo-mono-black.svg` | Single-color print, embossing, engraving |
| **Monochrome (white)** | `aiotize-logo-mono-white.svg` | Single-color print on dark substrates |
| **Icon only** | `aiotize-icon.svg` | App icons, favicons, social avatars |

---

## 📐 Clear Space

Always maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "A" in "Aiotize" (unit **X**).

```
         X
   ┌───────────────┐
 X │               │ X
   │   [LOGO]      │
 X │               │ X
   └───────────────┘
         X
```

**⚠️ Important:** No other graphic elements, text, or photography should appear within this clear space.

---

## 📏 Minimum Sizes

| Medium | Minimum Width |
|--------|--------------|
| 🖥️ Digital (screen) | 80 px |
| 🖨️ Print | 20 mm |
| 🔳 Icon-only mark | 16 px (digital) / 6 mm (print) |

> Below these sizes, the logo becomes illegible and should not be used.

---

## 🎨 Approved Background Colors

| Background | Permitted Variant |
|------------|------------------|
| White (`#FFFFFF`) | Primary color, Monochrome black |
| Light gray (`#F5F5F5` or lighter) | Primary color, Monochrome black |
| Brand Navy (`#0D1B2A`) | Reversed (white), Monochrome white |
| Brand Blue (`#1A6BFF`) | Reversed (white), Monochrome white |
| Photography | Reversed (white) only, ensure sufficient contrast |

---

## ✅ Do / Don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|-------|---------|
| Use the provided SVG or PNG files unchanged | Recreate the logo in another tool |
| Maintain the required clear space | Crowd the logo with text or other elements |
| Use approved background color combinations | Place the logo on busy patterns or low-contrast backgrounds |
| Scale the logo proportionally | Stretch or compress the logo |
| Use the reversed variant on dark backgrounds | Use the full-color variant on a dark background |
| Use the icon mark for small formats | Use the full lockup at icon sizes |

---

## 🚫 Misuse Examples

- ❌ Rotating the logo
- ❌ Changing logo colors to unapproved values
- ❌ Adding drop shadows, outlines, or other effects
- ❌ Placing the logo inside a colored box or badge (use the logo directly)
- ❌ Using an outdated logo version

---

## 🤝 External Use

Partners and press may only use Aiotize logo assets obtained from the official press kit in [`brand/templates/`](../brand/templates/).

**Any other use requires written approval from brand@aiotize.com.**
